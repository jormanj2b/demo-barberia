    Questions

    FAQ

    What if I’m running late?

    We’ll hold your spot for 10 minutes. After that, if your barber has another client, we’ll move you to the next open time.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Yes, when a chair is free. Book ahead and you won’t wait.

    How do I pay?

    In the shop, by card, Apple Pay or cash. Tips are welcome on card or in cash.
